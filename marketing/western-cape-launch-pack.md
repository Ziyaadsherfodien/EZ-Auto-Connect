## Organic lead acquisition checklist

1. Ask existing customers and professional contacts for permission to share the launch message.
2. Build referral relationships with employers, community groups, driving schools and vehicle-related service providers; obtain permission before posting in their spaces.
3. Participate helpfully in Western Cape community and motoring groups; follow each group's promotional rules.
4. Publish practical finance education, not only sales posts.
5. Ask satisfied customers for honest reviews only after a real interaction; never buy or fabricate reviews.
6. Track every enquiry source in the CRM: WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Google, referral, community group or direct.
7. Follow up only in accordance with the customer's stated contact preferences and consent.

## Launch compliance and trust controls

- Use contact lists only where the person has consented or another lawful basis applies; honour opt-outs immediately.
- Keep dealership sharing consent separate, clear and recorded.
- Do not collect ID documents through public social comments or ordinary WhatsApp chats; direct customers to the authenticated secure portal when it is available.
- Do not request or publish bank statements, ID numbers, payslips or credit information in public channels.
- Never promise approval, guaranteed lowest instalment, guaranteed savings or a specific rate.
- Make clear that participating dealerships and finance providers make their own offers and lending decisions.
- Have the privacy notice, terms, consent wording, retention schedule and any applicable regulatory requirements reviewed before scaling.
