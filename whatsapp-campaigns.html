1. Import contacts

Upload an iPhone vCard (.vcf) or a WhatsApp contacts CSV/vCard export. Contacts are matched by phone number; existing records are preserved.

2. Record historic opt-in

Your confirmation is recorded as an administrator attestation. Keep the original opt-in evidence available for audit. This does not mark imported contacts opted in merely because they are in your address book.

3. Prepare campaign

WhatsApp Business Platform sends approved template messages for business-initiated campaigns. The template name and language below must match an approved MARKETING template in your WhatsApp Manager. A free-form message in this preview is not automatically a Meta-approved template. Your approved template must already exist in WhatsApp Manager.

4. Final approval

Approving locks this message and the audience snapshot. After approval, the system sends in batches through WhatsApp Business Platform. It rechecks consent and suppression before each send, and excludes anyone who opts out.
