EZ AUTO CONNECT

Privacy Notice

Version EZ-PRIVACY-2026-11-21-v1 · Effective 21 November 2026

EZ Auto Connect is operated by EZ Consulting. This notice explains how we collect, use, store and share personal information when you use our website, customer portal, dealership portal and related vehicle enquiry and finance-comparison services.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the services you request, we may process your name, contact details, identity and verification documents, address and location information, vehicle preferences, budget and affordability information, employment and income documents, finance quotations and decisions, dealership communications, and records of your choices, consents and interactions. We may also collect technical information needed to secure and operate the platform.

2. Why we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts; verify identity; receive and manage vehicle enquiries; identify suitable participating dealerships; request, receive and compare dealership quotations; communicate service updates using your selected channel; provide customer support; administer dealership accounts, invoices and commissions; prevent fraud and protect the platform; maintain records; and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

3. Sharing and recipients

Where relevant to an enquiry and permitted by law, information may be shared with the dealership or dealerships you ask us to approach, service providers that host or support the platform, and professional or regulatory parties where legally required. Credit bureau information will only be requested where a lawful purpose, the required specific authorisation and an approved provider integration are in place. Insurance information will only be disclosed to a named insurer or intermediary where the required specific permission has been obtained. A general acceptance of this notice does not replace any separate authorisation required for a particular credit bureau enquiry or insurance referral.

4. Communication preference

During registration you select either WhatsApp or email as your preferred channel for service and vehicle-finance updates. We use that selection for operational communications relating to your account and enquiries. You may change the selected channel in your customer profile or contact us. This channel preference is not a separate opt-in to unrelated marketing. Where marketing consent is required, it will be requested separately.

5. Storage and security

We use access controls, authentication, private document storage and audit records intended to protect personal information. Access is limited according to role and business need. No electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure; please notify us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

6. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice, to complete requested services, resolve disputes, maintain appropriate business records and comply with applicable legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of record and applicable requirements. When information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted, de-identified or otherwise disposed of where appropriate and lawful.

7. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction of your personal information, object to or withdraw a permission where applicable, request deletion where legally permitted, and raise a complaint with the Information Regulator. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal or processing that must continue under law.

8. Children

The service is intended for adults arranging vehicle enquiries and finance comparisons. Do not submit another person's information unless you are authorised to do so.

9. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or complaints, contact EZ Auto Connect at ziyaad@ezconsulting.co.za. Please provide enough detail for us to identify the relevant account without sending passwords or unnecessary identity documents by ordinary email.

10. Changes

We may update this notice when our services or legal obligations change. The version and effective date will be displayed on this page. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel.

This notice is a working operational draft and should be reviewed by a South African privacy/legal professional before public launch, especially to confirm the responsible party's registered details, Information Officer details, retention schedule, operator arrangements and complaint process.
